01 / 07 Beginning

THE LAST LIBRARY LIGHT

After Closing

Mira stays in the library after her last class, pretending to finish a page she has read three times.

Outside, groups of students hurry through the rain together.

She knows her way around campus now, but she has not found a place that feels like hers.

02 / 07 Beginning

The Invitation

A narrow slip of paper falls from the book beside her.

Someone has written, 'If you are lost, find the light we leave for you,' above a tiny drawing of a lantern.

Mira almost leaves it on the desk, then notices the same symbol on an old catalog drawer.

03 / 07 Middle

Names in the Drawer

The drawer holds cards left by students who once felt just as new.

One describes a first day without a familiar face; another remembers eating lunch alone.

Behind them is a small map leading to a reading room Mira has never noticed.

04 / 07 Middle

A Place Kept Open

In the reading room, one lamp shines over an empty chair.

A sign says that a student reading circle meets here in the morning, and that a seat is always kept for someone new.

For the first time that evening, the silence feels like room to breathe.

05 / 07 Middle / turning point

What Will You Leave?

Beside the lamp are a map and a blank card.

Mira can follow the invitation tomorrow, or write a message for the next person who needs it.

The viewer chooses which small beginning she makes: follow the light, or leave a light.

06 / 07 Ending A

Follow the Light

In the morning, Mira returns with the map folded in her pocket.

At the reading-room door, someone moves a bag from the empty chair and smiles.

She has not become a different person overnight; she has simply accepted a place that was waiting for her.

07 / 07 Ending B

Leave a Light

Mira writes, 'Your first quiet week is not your whole story,' and slips the card into a book.

She leaves the lamp on for the next reader and walks outside with a lighter step.

The library is still unfamiliar, but now it holds something of hers.

A small beginning is still a beginning.
